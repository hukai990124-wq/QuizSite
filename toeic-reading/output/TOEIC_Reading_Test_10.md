**101. D** — Why: The fall in costs is a result of the early installation, so “consequently” is correct; “nevertheless” would wrongly signal a contrast between two positive facts.

**104. C** — Why: The clause after the gap is complete and describes a place (the harbor), so the relative adverb “where” is needed; “which” would require a missing subject or object in the clause.

**107. C** — Why: The reflexive pronoun “herself” is used for emphasis to mean that Ms. Albescu does the review personally; “her” and “hers” cannot stand alone after the object here.
