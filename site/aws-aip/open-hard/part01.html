Question 1Domain 1 · Task 1.1

A company runs a high-volume Amazon Bedrock summarization workload with steady, predictable traffic 24 hours a day. The team wants the lowest cost per token while guaranteeing that capacity is always available during business-critical hours. Following the Cost Optimization pillar of the Generative AI Lens, which approach is MOST appropriate?

一家公司每天 24 小时运行大量 Amazon Bedrock 汇总工作负载，流量稳定、可预测。该团队希望每个代币的成本最低，同时保证容量在业务关键时段始终可用。遵循生成式 AI 镜头的成本优化支柱，哪种方法最合适？

  1. A

    Purchase Provisioned Throughput with a committed term for the model.

    购买具有模型承诺期限的预配置吞吐量。

  2. B

    Use on-demand inference for every request.

    对每个请求使用按需推理。

  3. C

    Run the workload as a nightly Bedrock batch inference job.

    将工作负载作为每晚的 Bedrock 批量推理作业运行。

  4. D

    Increase max tokens per request so fewer calls are needed.

    增加每个请求的最大令牌数，从而减少需要的调用。

解析 / Explanation

正确答案 / Answer: A

A. For steady, high, predictable volume, a committed Provisioned Throughput model unit lowers cost per token and guarantees dedicated capacity.

B. On-demand pricing suits spiky or unpredictable low volume; at steady high volume it is not the lowest cost and does not reserve capacity.

C. Batch inference is for offline, latency-tolerant jobs; it cannot serve steady real-time traffic.

D. Larger outputs raise cost per call and do not reduce the number of distinct summaries needed.

A. 为了获得稳定、高、可预测的交易量，承诺的预配置吞吐量模型单位可以降低每个代币的成本并保证专用容量。

B. 按需定价适合尖峰或不可预测的小批量；在稳定的高产量下，它不是最低的成本，并且不保留产能。

C. 批量推理适用于离线、耐延迟的作业；它无法提供稳定的实时流量。

D. 较大的输出会增加每次调用的成本，并且不会减少所需的不同摘要的数量。
