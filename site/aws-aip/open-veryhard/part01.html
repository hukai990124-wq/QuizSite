Question 1Domain 1 · Task 1.1

A production Bedrock workload has steady, high, predictable demand for 12 business hours and near-zero traffic overnight. The team must guarantee dedicated capacity during business hours (no throttling) at the lowest total cost, keeping the same model and API. Which approach is BEST?

生产基岩工作负载在 12 个工作小时内具有稳定、高且可预测的需求，并且夜间流量接近于零。团队必须以最低的总成本保证工作时间内的专用容量（无节流），并保持相同的模型和 API。哪种方法最好？

  1. A

    Use Provisioned Throughput without a long-term commitment, creating model units for business hours and deleting them overnight.

    使用预置吞吐量而无需长期承诺，为工作时间创建模型单元并在一夜之间将其删除。

  2. B

    Use Provisioned Throughput with a 6-month commitment sized for peak.

    使用预置吞吐量和 6 个月的峰值承诺。

  3. C

    Use on-demand inference for all traffic.

    对所有流量使用按需推理。

  4. D

    Use cross-Region inference to add capacity during business hours.

    使用跨区域推理在工作时间内增加容量。

解析 / Explanation

正确答案 / Answer: A

A. No-commitment Provisioned Throughput guarantees dedicated capacity while active and is billed only while provisioned, so releasing units overnight avoids paying for the idle period.

B. A committed term is cheapest per hour but you pay for the reserved units overnight too, so with near-zero overnight traffic it is not the lowest total cost.

C. On-demand has no idle cost but cannot guarantee dedicated capacity during peak hours, so it fails the no-throttling requirement.

D. Cross-Region inference spreads on-demand traffic for throughput/availability but does not reserve guaranteed capacity, and it is still on-demand priced.

A. 无承诺预置吞吐量可保证活动时的专用容量，并且仅在预置时计费，因此隔夜释放单位可以避免为空闲期付费。

B. 承诺期限每小时最便宜，但您也需要为预留单位隔夜付费，因此，在夜间流量接近于零的情况下，这并不是最低的总成本。

C. 按需模式没有闲置成本，但无法保证高峰时段的专用容量，无法满足不限流的要求。

D. 跨区域推理会按需分配流量以提高吞吐量/可用性，但不会预留保证容量，并且仍按按需定价。

D. 输出长度对错误或区域故障的恢复能力没有影响。
