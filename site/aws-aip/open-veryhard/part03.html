A. Embeddings persist in the vector store until the knowledge base is updated, so both the source and the ingested vectors must be removed.

B. Deleting the source leaves the already-ingested embeddings in the vector store, so the data is not fully removed.

C. Logs are unrelated to the embeddings retained in the vector store.

D. Object Lock prevents deletion during retention, which is the opposite of fulfilling an erasure request.

A. 嵌入会一直保留在向量存储中，直到知识库更新为止，因此必须删除源向量和摄取的向量。

B. 删除源会将已提取的嵌入留在向量存储中，因此数据并未完全删除。

C. 日志与向量存储中保留的嵌入无关。

D. 对象锁定可防止在保留期间删除，这与满足擦除请求相反。
