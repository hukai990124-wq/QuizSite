PART 5 — Incomplete Sentences（101–130）

Directions: A word or phrase is missing in each of the sentences below. Four answer choices are given below each sentence. Select the best answer to complete the sentence. Then mark the letter (A), (B), (C), or (D) on your answer sheet.
101.The quarterly sales figures will be reviewed ____ the board meeting on Friday afternoon.
  1. Aamong
  2. Bbetween
  3. Conto
  4. Dduring
解析 / Explanation
D. 'During' shows the review happens within the time span of the meeting; the others cannot introduce an event like this.
102.The new scheduling software has significantly improved the ____ of our customer service team.
  1. Aefficient
  2. Befficiently
  3. Cefficiencies
  4. Defficiency
解析 / Explanation
D. A singular noun is needed after 'the' and before 'of', so 'efficiency' is correct.
103.Neither the department manager nor her assistants ____ available to comment on the merger.
  1. Ais
  2. Bwas
  3. Care
  4. Dhas
解析 / Explanation
C. With 'neither...nor', the verb agrees with the nearer subject 'assistants', which is plural, so 'are'.
104.Because the shipment was delayed, the company had to ____ its customers about the new delivery date.
  1. Ainform
  2. Bannounce
  3. Cmention
  4. Dexplain
解析 / Explanation
A. Only 'inform' takes a person as its direct object ('inform its customers about'); the others require a different structure.
105.The consultant ____ recommendations saved us thousands of dollars is presenting again today.
  1. Awho
  2. Bwhom
  3. Cwhich
  4. Dwhose
解析 / Explanation
D. The possessive relative pronoun 'whose' is needed before the noun 'recommendations'.
106.This year's turnout was far ____ than last year's, exceeding all of the organizers' expectations.
  1. Alarger
  2. Blarge
  3. Clargest
  4. Dlargely
解析 / Explanation
A. The word 'than' signals a comparison between two years, so the comparative 'larger' is required.
107.____ the proposal was submitted on time, the committee decided to postpone its final decision.
  1. AAlthough
  2. BBecause
  3. CUnless
  4. DSo
解析 / Explanation
A. The contrast between an on-time submission and a postponement calls for the concession word 'Although'.
108.The technicians ____ the equipment three times this week, but it keeps malfunctioning.
  1. Arepair
  2. Bhave repaired
  3. Crepairing
  4. Dwill repair
解析 / Explanation
B. A repeated action within the unfinished period 'this week' takes the present perfect 'have repaired'.
109.Despite the tight deadline, the contract negotiations proceeded ____ from start to finish.
  1. Asmooth
  2. Bsmoothly
  3. Csmoother
  4. Dsmoothness
解析 / Explanation
B. An adverb is needed to modify the verb 'proceeded', so 'smoothly' is correct.
110.Please submit your expense reports ____ the end of the month to ensure timely reimbursement.
  1. Aby
  2. Buntil
  3. Csince
  4. Dfor
解析 / Explanation
A. 'By' marks a deadline meaning 'no later than', whereas 'until' would imply a continuous action up to that point.
111.The board members praised the marketing team for ____ innovative and cost-effective campaign.
  1. Atheirs
  2. Bthem
  3. Cthey
  4. Dtheir
解析 / Explanation
D. A possessive adjective is needed before the noun 'campaign', so 'their' is correct.
112.The keynote address at the annual conference will be ____ by the company's chief executive.
  1. Adeliver
  2. Bdelivering
  3. Cdelivered
  4. Ddelivery
解析 / Explanation
C. The passive structure 'will be ____' requires the past participle 'delivered'.
113.The proposed merger will ____ nearly every department, so managers should prepare their teams.
  1. Aaffect
  2. Beffect
  3. Ceffective
  4. Daffection
解析 / Explanation
A. The verb 'affect' means 'to influence'; 'effect' as a verb means 'to bring about', which does not fit here.
114.The finance office is responsible for ____ the annual budget and reviewing all departmental spending.
  1. Aprepare
  2. Bprepares
  3. Cpreparing
  4. Dpreparation
解析 / Explanation
C. After the preposition 'for', a gerund is required, so 'preparing' is correct.
115.Each of the shortlisted applicants ____ required to complete a written assessment before the interview.
  1. Ais
  2. Bare
  3. Cwere
  4. Dhave
解析 / Explanation
A. The subject 'Each' is singular, so the singular verb 'is' is correct.
116.The store will remain open ____ the renovations on the ground floor are completely finished.
  1. Aduring
  2. Buntil
  3. Cdespite
  4. Dwhile
解析 / Explanation
B. 'Until' marks the point up to which the store stays open; 'during' and 'despite' cannot introduce a clause here.
117.The assembly instructions were so ____ that none of the new employees could follow them.
  1. Aconfuse
  2. Bconfused
  3. Cconfusing
  4. Dconfusion
解析 / Explanation
C. The '-ing' adjective 'confusing' describes something that causes confusion, which fits the instructions.
118.By the time the external auditors arrived, the accountant ____ all of the financial records.
  1. Aorganized
  2. Bhad organized
  3. Chas organized
  4. Dorganizes
解析 / Explanation
B. The past perfect 'had organized' shows the action was completed before another past event.
119.The company specializes ____ manufacturing environmentally friendly packaging for the food industry.
  1. Ain
  2. Bon
  3. Cat
  4. Dwith
解析 / Explanation
A. The verb 'specialize' is followed by the preposition 'in'.
120.Of all the candidates interviewed this week, she was clearly the ____ qualified for the position.
  1. Amore
  2. Bmost
  3. Cmuch
  4. Dvery
解析 / Explanation
B. Comparing one person against an entire group requires the superlative, so 'the most qualified'.
121.All managers are expected to ____ to the company's revised data-protection guidelines.
  1. Acomply
  2. Bobserve
  3. Cadhere
  4. Dfollow
解析 / Explanation
C. 'Adhere' correctly pairs with 'to'; 'comply' takes 'with', and 'observe' and 'follow' take a direct object without 'to'.
122.The interns completed the entire inventory project by ____ without any supervision.
  1. Athem
  2. Bthey
  3. Cthemselves
  4. Dtheir
解析 / Explanation
C. The reflexive pronoun 'themselves' is used in the phrase 'by themselves', meaning on their own.
123.The regional office is seeking an experienced ____ to lead its expanding finance department.
  1. Asupervise
  2. Bsupervisor
  3. Csupervisory
  4. Dsupervised
解析 / Explanation
B. A noun naming a person is needed after 'an experienced', so 'supervisor' is correct.
124.____ you have any questions about the new policy, please contact the human resources department.
  1. AIf
  2. BUnless
  3. CWhether
  4. DDespite
解析 / Explanation
A. A conditional meaning is required, so 'If' introduces the clause correctly.
125.Ticket prices for the trade show range ____ fifty to two hundred dollars depending on the package.
  1. Abetween
  2. Bamong
  3. Cof
  4. Dfrom
解析 / Explanation
D. The pattern 'range from...to...' requires 'from'; 'between' would need 'and' instead of 'to'.
126.We will notify you by email as soon as your order ____ from our distribution center.
  1. Ashipped
  2. Bwill ship
  3. Cshipping
  4. Dships
解析 / Explanation
D. In a time clause introduced by 'as soon as', the present simple 'ships' expresses future meaning.
127.The mobile application is ____ designed for users with limited technical experience.
  1. Aspecific
  2. Bspecify
  3. Cspecifically
  4. Dspecification
解析 / Explanation
C. An adverb is needed to modify the participle 'designed', so 'specifically' is correct.
128.The manager decided to ____ the meeting until all of the department heads could attend.
  1. Acancel
  2. Battend
  3. Cprevent
  4. Dpostpone
解析 / Explanation
D. 'Postpone...until' means to delay to a later time, which fits waiting for the department heads.
129.____ of the two proposals offers a decisive advantage, so the team will continue developing both.
  1. ANone
  2. BBoth
  3. CEither
  4. DNeither
解析 / Explanation
D. 'Neither' with a singular verb fits the logic that no single proposal stands out, so both are kept.
130.When ____ properly, the machine can operate for years with very little additional maintenance.
  1. Amaintain
  2. Bmaintained
  3. Cmaintaining
  4. Dmaintenance
解析 / Explanation
B. The reduced passive clause 'When (it is) maintained properly' requires the past participle 'maintained'.
