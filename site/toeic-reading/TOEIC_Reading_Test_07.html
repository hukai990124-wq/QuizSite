PART 5 — Incomplete Sentences（101–130）

Directions: A word or phrase is missing in each of the sentences below. Four answer choices are given below each sentence. Select the best answer to complete the sentence. Then mark the letter (A), (B), (C), or (D) on your answer sheet.
101.Your travel coordinator will send you a detailed ____ listing all flight times and hotel addresses.
  1. Ainventory
  2. Bagenda
  3. Citinerary
  4. Dinvoice
解析 / Explanation
C. An itinerary is a travel plan that lists flights and hotels, so (C) is correct. (A) 'inventory' is a list of stock, (B) 'agenda' is a list of topics for a meeting, and (D) 'invoice' is a bill.
102.Ms. Oyelaran ____ the hospital's volunteer program since it was founded in 2019.
  1. Ahas coordinated
  2. Bcoordinates
  3. Ccoordinated
  4. Dwill coordinate
解析 / Explanation
A. 'Since it was founded' describes a period that continues up to now, which requires the present perfect (A) 'has coordinated'. (B) 'coordinates' and (C) 'coordinated' cannot be used with 'since' in this way, and (D) 'will coordinate' is future.
103.Employees who have worked at Kestrel Logistics for at least six months are ____ for the tuition assistance program.
  1. Acapable
  2. Bconsiderable
  3. Centitled
  4. Deligible
解析 / Explanation
D. 'Eligible for' means meeting the conditions to take part, so (D) is correct. (A) 'capable' is followed by 'of', (C) 'entitled' is followed by 'to', and (B) 'considerable' means large in amount.
104.To ____ the printer's deadline, the design team divided the catalog pages among three editors.
  1. Aobey
  2. Bmeet
  3. Cattend
  4. Dcomply
解析 / Explanation
B. 'Meet a deadline' is the standard collocation, so (B) is correct. (A) 'obey' is used with rules or orders, (C) 'attend' is used with events, and (D) 'comply' needs 'with'.
105.The owner of the food truck has applied for a ____ to serve customers along the riverside promenade on weekends.
  1. Apermit
  2. Bpermitted
  3. Cpermissive
  4. Dpermissible
解析 / Explanation
A. After the article 'a', a noun is needed, and (A) 'permit' is an official document that allows an activity. (B) 'permitted' is a verb form, and (C) 'permissive' and (D) 'permissible' are adjectives with no noun to describe.
106.____ the client approves the revised layout by this afternoon, the brochures can still be printed before the trade fair.
  1. AUnless
  2. BAlthough
  3. CProvided that
  4. DWhereas
解析 / Explanation
C. (C) 'Provided that' means 'on the condition that': if approval comes today, printing is still possible. (A) 'Unless' reverses this logic, and (B) 'Although' and (D) 'Whereas' express contrast, which does not fit.
107.Every runner who completes the half-marathon will receive a ____ T-shirt and a finisher's medal.
  1. Acomplicated
  2. Bcompetent
  3. Ccompulsory
  4. Dcomplimentary
解析 / Explanation
D. 'Complimentary' means given free of charge, which suits a gift for every finisher, so (D) is correct. (A) 'complicated' means difficult, (B) 'competent' means skilled, and (C) 'compulsory' means required.
108.The storage facility, ____ is located just off the ring road, offers climate-controlled units for artwork and wine.
  1. Awho
  2. Bwhich
  3. Cwhere
  4. Dwhat
解析 / Explanation
B. A relative pronoun for a thing that is also the subject of 'is located' is needed, so (B) 'which' is correct. (A) 'who' refers to people, (C) 'where' cannot act as a subject, and (D) 'what' cannot refer back to a noun.
109.Because the delay was caused by the bank's own error, it has agreed to ____ the late-payment fee.
  1. Aimpose
  2. Bcollect
  3. Caccrue
  4. Dwaive
解析 / Explanation
D. To 'waive' a fee is to cancel it, which is a fair response to the bank's own mistake, so (D) is correct. (A) 'impose' and (B) 'collect' would mean charging the fee, and (C) 'accrue' means to build up over time.
110.The clinic's new poster campaign aims to ____ awareness of the importance of regular eye examinations.
  1. Araise
  2. Brise
  3. Carise
  4. Dgather
解析 / Explanation
A. 'Raise awareness' is a fixed collocation, so (A) is correct. (B) 'rise' and (C) 'arise' are intransitive verbs that cannot take an object, and (D) 'gather' is not used with 'awareness'.
111.Hotel guests have found the new self-service check-in kiosks surprisingly ____ to use.
  1. Aintuition
  2. Bintuitively
  3. Cintuitive
  4. Dintuit
解析 / Explanation
C. In the pattern 'find + object + adjective', an adjective is needed, so (C) 'intuitive' is correct. (B) 'intuitively' is an adverb (the adverb 'surprisingly' already modifies the adjective), (A) 'intuition' is a noun, and (D) 'intuit' is a verb.
112.The number of passengers using the airport's express rail link ____ doubled since the fares were reduced.
  1. Ahave
  2. Bhas
  3. Care
  4. Dwere
解析 / Explanation
B. 'The number of' is a singular subject, so (B) 'has' is correct, forming the present perfect 'has doubled'. (A) 'have' wrongly agrees with 'passengers', and (C) 'are' and (D) 'were' do not form the needed tense.
113.The renovated city hall was designed to ____ wheelchair users, with ramps and wide doorways on every floor.
  1. Aaccommodate
  2. Baccompany
  3. Cacquire
  4. Daccumulate
解析 / Explanation
A. To 'accommodate' wheelchair users is to provide for their needs, so (A) is correct. (B) 'accompany' means to go with someone, (C) 'acquire' means to obtain, and (D) 'accumulate' means to collect gradually.
114.Ms. Takahashi prefers to review the supplier contracts ____ rather than assign the task to an assistant.
  1. Ashe
  2. Bher
  3. Chers
  4. Dherself
解析 / Explanation
D. The reflexive pronoun (D) 'herself' shows that Ms. Takahashi does the review personally. (A) 'she' is a subject pronoun, and (B) 'her' and (C) 'hers' cannot be added after a complete clause to give this meaning.
115.The dates for the regional sales summit are still ____ and may change once the venue is confirmed.
  1. Adefinite
  2. Bpunctual
  3. Ctentative
  4. Dreluctant
解析 / Explanation
C. Dates that 'may change' are not final, so (C) 'tentative' is correct. (A) 'definite' means the opposite, (B) 'punctual' means on time, and (D) 'reluctant' describes people who are unwilling.
116.The free downtown shuttle bus runs every twenty minutes ____ 7:00 A.M. and 11:00 P.M.
  1. Abetween
  2. Bamong
  3. Cwithin
  4. Dthroughout
解析 / Explanation
A. The pattern 'between ... and ...' marks a period with two end points, so (A) is correct. (B) 'among' is used with groups, and (C) 'within' and (D) 'throughout' are not paired with 'and' in this way.
117.The research firm will ____ a series of interviews with frequent business travelers to learn how they choose hotels.
  1. Areserve
  2. Bconduct
  3. Cadmit
  4. Dbook
解析 / Explanation
B. Researchers 'conduct' interviews, meaning they carry them out, so (B) is correct. (A) 'reserve' and (D) 'book' are used for rooms, seats, or tickets, and (C) 'admit' means to let in or to confess.
118.Pre-orders for the folding electric scooter have already ____ the manufacturer's forecast for the entire year.
  1. Aexcelled
  2. Bexited
  3. Cextended
  4. Dexceeded
解析 / Explanation
D. Numbers that go beyond a forecast have 'exceeded' it, so (D) is correct. (A) 'excelled' means performed very well and does not take this object, (B) 'exited' means left, and (C) 'extended' means made longer.
119.The board is considering ____ a second warehouse closer to the port to reduce delivery times.
  1. Alease
  2. Bto lease
  3. Cleasing
  4. Dleased
解析 / Explanation
C. The verb 'consider' is followed by a gerund, so (C) 'leasing' is correct. (B) 'to lease' is a common error after 'consider', and (A) 'lease' and (D) 'leased' cannot follow 'considering'.
120.The architects' revised floor plan is ____ similar to the version they presented in March.
  1. Aremark
  2. Bremarkable
  3. Cremarked
  4. Dremarkably
解析 / Explanation
D. An adverb is needed to modify the adjective 'similar', so (D) 'remarkably' is correct. (B) 'remarkable' is an adjective, (A) 'remark' is a noun or verb, and (C) 'remarked' is a past verb form.
121.Ms. Kowalczyk accepted the sustainability award ____ the entire logistics department.
  1. Ain charge of
  2. Bon behalf of
  3. Cin favor of
  4. Dregardless of
解析 / Explanation
B. Accepting an award 'on behalf of' a group means doing so as its representative, so (B) is correct. (A) 'in charge of' means managing, (C) 'in favor of' means supporting, and (D) 'regardless of' means without considering.
122.The winners of this year's young chefs' competition ____ at a ceremony in Lisbon next month.
  1. Awill be announced
  2. Bwill announce
  3. Care announcing
  4. Dannounced
解析 / Explanation
A. The winners do not announce anything; they are announced, so a passive verb is needed, and 'next month' requires the future: (A) 'will be announced'. (B) and (C) are active, and (D) is past.
123.Audience members are asked to ____ from using their phones while the performance is in progress.
  1. Aavoid
  2. Bprevent
  3. Crefrain
  4. Dprohibit
解析 / Explanation
C. Only 'refrain' is followed directly by 'from' + -ing with no object, so (C) is correct. (A) 'avoid' takes a gerund without 'from', and (B) 'prevent' and (D) 'prohibit' need an object before 'from'.
124.The building manager has requested that each tenant ____ the updated fire-safety checklist before Friday.
  1. Acompletes
  2. Bcompleted
  3. Ccompleting
  4. Dcomplete
解析 / Explanation
D. After 'request that', the base form of the verb is used for every subject, so (D) 'complete' is correct. (A) 'completes' is a common error here, (B) 'completed' is past tense, and (C) 'completing' is not a finite verb.
125.____ at the free lunchtime concerts in Bellmont Square has risen every summer since the series began.
  1. AAttendance
  2. BAttention
  3. CAttendant
  4. DAppearance
解析 / Explanation
A. The number of people who go to an event is its 'attendance', so (A) is correct. (C) 'Attendant' is a person who helps others, (B) 'Attention' means focus, and (D) 'Appearance' refers to being seen or to how something looks.
126.The new meeting room on the fifth floor holds twice as many people ____ the old one did.
  1. Athan
  2. Bso
  3. Cas
  4. Dlike
解析 / Explanation
C. The structure 'twice as many ... as' requires (C) 'as'. (A) 'than' follows comparative forms such as 'more', and (B) 'so' and (D) 'like' cannot complete this comparison.
127.Restaurants that ____ an order with Delvaux Produce by 9 P.M. receive their delivery the next morning.
  1. Afill
  2. Bplace
  3. Cset
  4. Dhand
解析 / Explanation
B. Customers 'place' an order with a supplier, so (B) is correct. (A) 'fill' an order is what the supplier does, and (C) 'set' and (D) 'hand' are not used with 'order' in this sense.
128.Before choosing a contractor, the manager of the climbing gym requested a written ____ for resurfacing the entrance ramp.
  1. Areceipt
  2. Bstatement
  3. Crefund
  4. Destimate
解析 / Explanation
D. A written 'estimate' states the expected cost of work before it begins, so (D) is correct. (A) 'receipt' is given after payment, (B) 'statement' summarizes an account, and (C) 'refund' is money paid back.
129.If an applicant's forms are incomplete, the registration office will return ____ with a checklist of the missing items.
  1. Athey
  2. Bthem
  3. Ctheir
  4. Dthemselves
解析 / Explanation
B. An object pronoun is needed after 'return' to replace 'forms', so (B) 'them' is correct. (A) 'they' is a subject pronoun, (C) 'their' must be followed by a noun, and (D) 'themselves' would refer back to the subject.
130.The east entrance will be ____ closed while the new revolving door is installed.
  1. Atemporarily
  2. Bformerly
  3. Cpreviously
  4. Deventually
解析 / Explanation
A. The entrance is closed only for as long as the installation takes, so (A) 'temporarily' is correct. (B) 'formerly' and (C) 'previously' refer to the past, and (D) 'eventually' means in the end, which does not match 'while'.
