PART 5 — Incomplete Sentences（101–130）

Directions: A word or phrase is missing in each of the sentences below. Four answer choices are given below each sentence. Select the best answer to complete the sentence. Then mark the letter (A), (B), (C), or (D) on your answer sheet.
101.Visitors to the Tavira Energy plant must sign in at the security desk located _____ the main entrance.
  1. Aamong
  2. Bbeside
  3. Cbetween
  4. Dthroughout
解析 / Explanation
B. Why: “Beside” means “next to” and correctly locates the desk in relation to one entrance; “between” needs two points of reference, “among” needs a group, and “throughout” means “in every part of”.
102.Applications for the regional agricultural innovation grant must be submitted no later than 5:00 P.M. _____ Friday, 14 March.
  1. Ain
  2. Bat
  3. Cduring
  4. Don
解析 / Explanation
D. Why: Days and dates take “on” (on Friday, 14 March); “at” is used for clock times such as 5:00 P.M., which is the trap, and “in” is used for months and years.
103.Since the start of the year, Veltro Motors _____ its production of electric delivery vans by nearly 30 percent.
  1. Aincreases
  2. Bwill increase
  3. Chas increased
  4. Dincreasing
解析 / Explanation
C. Why: “Since the start of the year” describes a period that began in the past and continues to now, so the present perfect “has increased” is needed; “increasing” is not a finite verb.
104.The marketing team worked _____ to finish the campaign materials before the product launch on Monday.
  1. Anarrowly
  2. Bvaguely
  3. Csparsely
  4. Dtirelessly
解析 / Explanation
D. Why: “Worked tirelessly” (worked very hard without stopping) is a natural collocation that fits a deadline context; “narrowly”, “vaguely” and “sparsely” do not describe how someone works toward a goal.
105._____ the conference hall was fully booked, the organizers arranged a second room with a live video feed.
  1. AAlthough
  2. BBecause
  3. CUnless
  4. DWhereas
解析 / Explanation
B. Why: The first clause gives the reason for the second (the hall was full, so a second room was arranged), so “Because” is correct; “Although” would wrongly signal a contrast.
106.Each of the tour packages _____ airport transfers, daily breakfast, and a guided walk through the old town.
  1. Aincludes
  2. Binclude
  3. Cincluding
  4. Dare included
解析 / Explanation
A. Why: The subject is “Each”, which is singular, so the singular verb “includes” is required; “include” agrees with the nearby plural “packages”, which is the trap.
107.All customer complaints about dropped calls _____ to the network engineering team within 24 hours.
  1. Aforward
  2. Bare forwarded
  3. Care forwarding
  4. Dhave forwarded
解析 / Explanation
B. Why: Complaints do not forward anything; they are sent by someone, so the passive “are forwarded” is needed. The active forms “forward” and “have forwarded” would need an object.
108.The hotel’s general manager expressed her _____ to the staff for their hard work during the busy holiday season.
  1. Aappreciate
  2. Bappreciation
  3. Cappreciative
  4. Dappreciatively
解析 / Explanation
B. Why: After the possessive “her”, a noun is required, so “appreciation” is correct; the other choices are a verb, an adjective and an adverb.
109.Farmers _____ crops were damaged by last month’s hailstorm may apply for emergency assistance through the regional office.
  1. Awho
  2. Bwhom
  3. Cwhich
  4. Dwhose
解析 / Explanation
D. Why: The crops belong to the farmers, so the possessive relative pronoun “whose” is needed before the noun “crops”; “who”, “whom” and “which” cannot introduce a noun in this way.
110._____ rising fuel costs, Palmera Tours has raised the price of its coastal bus excursions by five dollars.
  1. ADue to
  2. BIn spite of
  3. CInstead of
  4. DAs far as
解析 / Explanation
A. Why: Rising fuel costs are the cause of the price increase, so “Due to” is correct; “In spite of” would suggest the price rose despite the costs, which is illogical.
111.The new billing software has been _____ tested to make sure that customer data remains secure.
  1. Amutually
  2. Bthoroughly
  3. Cevenly
  4. Dshortly
解析 / Explanation
B. Why: “Thoroughly tested” (tested completely and carefully) is a standard collocation; “shortly” means “soon” and does not describe how testing is done.
112.Registered attendees may pick up their badges _____ at the information desk or at one of the self-service kiosks in the lobby.
  1. Aeither
  2. Bneither
  3. Cboth
  4. Dnot only
解析 / Explanation
A. Why: The pair is completed by “or”, so “either” is needed; “neither” pairs with “nor”, “both” with “and”, and “not only” with “but also”.
113.Of all the solar panel models tested, the Helion X4 produced the _____ amount of electricity on cloudy days.
  1. Agreater
  2. Bgreatest
  3. Cgreat
  4. Dmore greatly
解析 / Explanation
B. Why: “Of all the models” and “the” signal a comparison among more than two items, so the superlative “greatest” is required; “greater” compares only two.
114.Guests have described the staff at the Coralina Resort as extremely _____ and quick to respond to requests.
  1. Ahelpful
  2. Bhelp
  3. Chelpfully
  4. Dhelpfulness
解析 / Explanation
A. Why: After “described … as extremely”, an adjective is needed, parallel with “quick”; “helpfully” is an adverb and “helpfulness” is a noun.
115.The results of the customer survey were _____ , as more than 90 percent of respondents rated the service as excellent.
  1. Aencourage
  2. Bencouraged
  3. Cencouraging
  4. Dencouragement
解析 / Explanation
C. Why: Results cause a feeling in others, so the -ing adjective “encouraging” is correct; “encouraged” describes a person who feels encouragement.
116.Mr. Balogun prefers to review the supplier contracts _____ rather than rely on a summary from the legal team.
  1. Ahimself
  2. Bhim
  3. Chis
  4. Dhe
解析 / Explanation
A. Why: "Himself" is an emphatic reflexive: Mr. Balogun reads the contracts personally instead of relying on the legal team. "Him" and "he" cannot stand alone after the object "the supplier contracts," and "his" would need a noun after it.
117.Every Veltro vehicle comes with a _____ warranty that covers all parts and labor for five years.
  1. Anumerous
  2. Bcomprehensive
  3. Cspacious
  4. Dfrequent
解析 / Explanation
B. Why: A “comprehensive warranty” is one that covers everything, which matches “covers all parts and labor”; “numerous” needs a plural noun, and “spacious” and “frequent” do not describe a warranty.
118._____ offering discounted rail passes, the city tourism board provides free walking maps at every major station.
  1. ABecause of
  2. BIn addition to
  3. CAccording to
  4. DOn behalf of
解析 / Explanation
B. Why: The board does two things (offers passes and provides maps), so “In addition to” is correct; “Because of” would wrongly present the passes as the cause of the maps.
119._____ employee must complete the online safety course before entering the turbine maintenance area.
  1. AAll
  2. BFew
  3. CEvery
  4. DMost
解析 / Explanation
C. Why: The singular noun “employee” and the singular verb “must complete” require “Every”; “All”, “Few” and “Most” are used with plural countable nouns.
120.If the shipment of seed potatoes _____ by Thursday, the farm cooperative will begin planting next week.
  1. Aarrived
  2. Bwill arrive
  3. Cwould have arrived
  4. Darrives
解析 / Explanation
D. Why: In a first conditional, the if-clause takes the present simple while the main clause uses “will”, so “arrives” is correct; “will arrive” is not used after “if” here.
121.The new mobile plan offers unlimited data; _____ , it costs less than our previous premium package.
  1. Aotherwise
  2. Binstead
  3. Ctherefore
  4. Dmoreover
解析 / Explanation
D. Why: The second clause adds another advantage to the first, so “moreover” is correct; “therefore” would wrongly suggest that unlimited data causes the lower price.
122.Only a _____ of the vehicles recalled last spring still need to be inspected at authorized dealerships.
  1. Alittle
  2. Bmuch
  3. Cfew
  4. Dseveral
解析 / Explanation
C. Why: “Vehicles” is a plural countable noun, so “a few of” is correct; “a little” and “much” are used with uncountable nouns, and “several” cannot follow “a”.
123.The event planner hopes to _____ costs by booking the venue and catering from a single supplier.
  1. Aminimize
  2. Bminimal
  3. Cminimum
  4. Dminimally
解析 / Explanation
A. Why: After “hopes to”, a base-form verb that can take the object “costs” is needed, so “minimize” is correct; the other choices are an adjective, a noun/adjective and an adverb.
124.The upgraded fiber network allows customers to download large files _____ faster than before.
  1. Aconsiderable
  2. Bconsider
  3. Cconsideration
  4. Dconsiderably
解析 / Explanation
D. Why: An adverb is needed to modify the comparative “faster”, so “considerably” is correct; “considerable” is an adjective and cannot modify another modifier.
125.The regional energy authority has agreed to _____ the approval process for small wind farm projects so that permits can be issued within weeks rather than months.
  1. Aprolong
  2. Bconsume
  3. Cresemble
  4. Dstreamline
解析 / Explanation
D. Why: "Streamline" means to make a process simpler and faster, which matches permits being issued "within weeks rather than months"; "prolong" (make longer) is the opposite, and "consume" and "resemble" do not fit "the approval process."
126.Before signing the agreement, Ms. Quispe asked the supplier to _____ the delivery terms outlined in section 4.
  1. Aclarify
  2. Brespond
  3. Cagree
  4. Darrive
解析 / Explanation
A. Why: “Clarify the delivery terms” means make them clearer, and “clarify” takes a direct object; “respond”, “agree” and “arrive” need a preposition (respond to, agree to/on) or take no object.
127.The hotel’s rooftop restaurant will be closed for repairs until May. _____ , guests may enjoy dinner in the lobby café.
  1. AOtherwise
  2. BSimilarly
  3. CIn the meantime
  4. DFor example
解析 / Explanation
C. Why: The second sentence tells guests what to do during the period before the restaurant reopens, so “In the meantime” is correct; “Otherwise” would mean “if not”, which does not fit.
128.By the time the trade fair opens next month, our team _____ all of the promotional displays.
  1. Aassembled
  2. Bhas assembled
  3. Cwill have assembled
  4. Dis assembling
解析 / Explanation
C. Why: “By the time … next month” refers to an action completed before a future point, so the future perfect “will have assembled” is correct; “assembled” and “has assembled” do not refer to the future.
129.Given the _____ demand for its fiber service, Brightline Telecom plans to double the number of its installation crews by June.
  1. Asurging
  2. Btentative
  3. Creluctant
  4. Dmarginal
解析 / Explanation
A. Why: "Surging demand" (demand that is rising quickly) explains why the company would double its crews; "marginal" (very small) and "tentative" (uncertain) demand would not justify expansion, and "reluctant" describes people, not demand.
130._____ the new booking system been tested more thoroughly, the double reservations last weekend could have been avoided.
  1. AShould
  2. BIf
  3. CHad
  4. DWere
解析 / Explanation
C. Why: This is an inverted third conditional (= If the system had been tested…): "been" needs "had," and "could have been avoided" refers to the past. "If" would need "had" after it, "Should" is used for future possibilities, and "Were" cannot be followed by "been."
