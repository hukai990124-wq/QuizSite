PART 5 — Incomplete Sentences（101–130）

Directions: A word or phrase is missing in each of the sentences below. Four answer choices are given below each sentence. Select the best answer to complete the sentence. Then mark the letter (A), (B), (C), or (D) on your answer sheet.
101.The keynote speaker, _____ presentation on sustainable packaging drew the largest crowd of the conference, has been invited back next year.
  1. Awho
  2. Bwhom
  3. Cwhich
  4. Dwhose
解析 / Explanation
D. Why: The gap is followed by a noun (presentation) that belongs to the speaker, so the possessive relative pronoun “whose” is needed; “who” and “whom” cannot come directly before a noun like this.
102._____ the fabric shipment arrived two days late, the boutique was still able to launch its autumn collection on schedule.
  1. ABecause
  2. BUnless
  3. CUntil
  4. DAlthough
解析 / Explanation
D. Why: The two clauses contrast (late delivery vs. launch on schedule), so the concessive conjunction “Although” fits; “Because” would wrongly present the delay as the reason for the on-time launch.
103.Passengers were _____ to learn that the new express rail service would reduce their travel time by almost half.
  1. Apleased
  2. Bpleasing
  3. Cplease
  4. Dpleasure
解析 / Explanation
A. Why: People who feel an emotion are described with the -ed participle, so “pleased” is correct; “pleasing” describes the thing that causes the feeling.
104.Ms. Okafor has asked the design team to _____ the brochure layout before it is sent to the printer on Thursday.
  1. Afinalize
  2. Bfinal
  3. Cfinally
  4. Dfinality
解析 / Explanation
A. Why: After “to” in “asked … to ___ the layout”, a base-form verb that takes an object is required, so “finalize” is correct; the other choices are an adjective, an adverb and a noun.
105.Visitors may pay for parking at the machine next to the main entrance _____ through the Harbor Mall mobile application.
  1. Anor
  2. Bso
  3. Cor
  4. Dbut
解析 / Explanation
C. Why: The sentence presents two alternative ways to pay, so “or” is correct; “nor” requires a negative such as “neither”, and “but” and “so” do not express alternatives.
106.The list of approved suppliers _____ updated every quarter by the purchasing department and posted on the company intranet.
  1. Aare
  2. Bis
  3. Cbe
  4. Dbeing
解析 / Explanation
B. Why: The subject is the singular noun “list” (not “suppliers”), so the singular verb “is” is needed; “are” is the trap that agrees with the nearer plural noun.
107.Applicants for the regional sales manager position must submit _____ a cover letter and two letters of reference by March 15.
  1. Aeither
  2. Bneither
  3. Cwhether
  4. Dboth
解析 / Explanation
D. Why: The pair is joined by “and”, which goes with “both”; “either” pairs with “or” and “neither” with “nor”.
108.All safety inspections at the Dunmore Bridge construction site must _____ by a certified structural engineer.
  1. Aconduct
  2. Bconducting
  3. Chave conducted
  4. Dbe conducted
解析 / Explanation
D. Why: Inspections do not conduct anything; they are conducted by an engineer, so the passive “be conducted” is required after “must”.
109.The latest software update is expected to significantly _____ the time needed to process customer refund requests.
  1. Ashorten
  2. Bshort
  3. Cshortly
  4. Dshortness
解析 / Explanation
A. Why: After “to” (and the adverb “significantly”) a verb taking the object “the time” is needed, so “shorten” is correct.
110.Mr. Delgado usually prefers to book _____ own flights rather than use the travel agency recommended by the company.
  1. Ahim
  2. Bhe
  3. Chis
  4. Dhimself
解析 / Explanation
C. Why: Only a possessive adjective can come before “own flights”, so “his” is correct; “himself” is a reflexive pronoun and cannot modify a noun.
111.The hotel manager agreed _____ the conference room rental fee for groups of more than fifty guests.
  1. Ato reduce
  2. Breducing
  3. Creduce
  4. Dreduced
解析 / Explanation
A. Why: The verb “agree” is followed by a to-infinitive, so “to reduce” is correct; “agreed reducing” is ungrammatical.
112._____ employee who attends Friday’s data security workshop will receive a certificate of completion from the IT department.
  1. AEvery
  2. BAll
  3. CMost
  4. DSeveral
解析 / Explanation
A. Why: The noun “employee” is singular and the verb is “attends”, so “Every” is needed; “All”, “Most” and “Several” require a plural noun.
113.Varentis Pharmaceuticals has received _____ from national regulators to begin selling its new allergy medication next spring.
  1. Aattendance
  2. Bapproach
  3. Capology
  4. Dapproval
解析 / Explanation
D. Why: Companies receive “approval” from regulators before selling a product; “approach” looks similar but does not make sense here.
114.The marketing director praised the event team for its careful _____ to detail during the product launch in Seoul.
  1. Aattentive
  2. Battention
  3. Cattentively
  4. Dattend
解析 / Explanation
B. Why: After the adjective “careful” a noun is needed, and the fixed expression is “attention to detail”.
115.Tickets for the Milan Designers’ Showcase sold out _____ after they became available on the event’s Web site.
  1. Aclosely
  2. Bhighly
  3. Cshortly
  4. Dnearly
解析 / Explanation
C. Why: “Shortly after” means “soon after” and is a common collocation; “nearly after” and “closely after” are not idiomatic here.
116.The new staff lounge is located _____ the cafeteria and the mail room on the second floor of the building.
  1. Aamong
  2. Bbetween
  3. Cacross
  4. Dalong
解析 / Explanation
B. Why: “Between” is used with two things joined by “and” (the cafeteria and the mail room); “among” is used for three or more.
117.If the contractor _____ the flooring materials by Friday, the renovation of the showroom will be completed on time.
  1. Adelivered
  2. Bdelivers
  3. Cwould deliver
  4. Dhad delivered
解析 / Explanation
B. Why: The main clause uses “will be completed”, so this is a first conditional and the if-clause takes the simple present “delivers”.
118.Ms. Petrova’s proposal to open a second warehouse was _____ received by the board, which approved it without changes.
  1. Aenthusiastic
  2. Benthusiastically
  3. Centhusiasm
  4. Denthusiast
解析 / Explanation
B. Why: An adverb is needed to modify the verb “was received”, so “enthusiastically” is correct.
119.Since joining Carrow Engineering in 2019, Mr. Tanaka _____ three major bridge projects in the region.
  1. Amanages
  2. Bwill manage
  3. Cis managing
  4. Dhas managed
解析 / Explanation
D. Why: “Since + past point” describes a period continuing up to now, so the present perfect “has managed” is required.
120._____ heavy snowfall, all afternoon flights from Oslo to Helsinki have been delayed by at least two hours.
  1. ADue to
  2. BIn addition to
  3. CInstead of
  4. DIn spite of
解析 / Explanation
A. Why: The snowfall is the cause of the delays, so “Due to” fits; “In spite of” expresses the opposite relationship.
121.The newly renovated Harbor Hotel offers _____ more spacious guest rooms than any other hotel in the downtown area.
  1. Avery
  2. Btoo
  3. Cconsiderably
  4. Dsuch
解析 / Explanation
C. Why: Only a degree adverb such as "considerably" (or much, far) can strengthen a comparative like "more spacious"; "very more" and "too more" are ungrammatical, and "such" needs a noun phrase ("such spacious rooms").
122.Please _____ the attached invoice carefully and let us know if you have any questions about the charges.
  1. Anotify
  2. Breview
  3. Creply
  4. Drespond
解析 / Explanation
B. Why: You “review” a document; “reply” and “respond” need “to”, and you “notify” a person, not an invoice.
123.By the time the new factory in Monterrey opens next spring, the company _____ more than two hundred workers.
  1. Ahas hired
  2. Bhad hired
  3. Chires
  4. Dwill have hired
解析 / Explanation
D. Why: “By the time … next spring” refers to an action completed before a future point, so the future perfect “will have hired” is needed.
124.The quarterly sales figures _____ to all regional managers yesterday afternoon, along with the new targets.
  1. Awere sent
  2. Bsent
  3. Chave sent
  4. Dare sending
解析 / Explanation
A. Why: The figures are the things being sent, and “yesterday” requires the past, so the past passive “were sent” is correct.
125.The museum’s special exhibition on maritime trade will remain open _____ the end of October.
  1. Aby
  2. Buntil
  3. Cwithin
  4. Dfor
解析 / Explanation
B. Why: "Remain open" describes a continuing situation, so "until" marks when it ends. "By" is the trap: it gives a deadline for a completed action ("finish by the end of October"), not the end point of an ongoing state.
126.The organizing committee is considering _____ the annual logistics conference to a larger venue in Singapore.
  1. Amoving
  2. Bto move
  3. Cmove
  4. Dmoved
解析 / Explanation
A. Why: The verb “consider” is followed by a gerund, so “moving” is correct.
127.Assembly Line 2 has been running _____ since the technicians replaced its faulty sensors last week.
  1. Areluctantly
  2. Btightly
  3. Cflawlessly
  4. Dconsiderably
解析 / Explanation
C. Why: A machine that has been repaired is now running "flawlessly" (without any problems). "Reluctantly" describes a person's attitude, and "tightly" and "considerably" do not describe how a machine operates.
128.Online orders placed before noon are _____ for same-day delivery to addresses within the city limits.
  1. Acapable
  2. Bresponsible
  3. Celigible
  4. Dreliable
解析 / Explanation
C. Why: The collocation is “eligible for” (meeting the conditions for something); “capable” takes “of”, and “responsible” and “reliable” do not fit the meaning.
129.The client requested several changes to the floor plan; _____ , the architects revised the drawings within a week.
  1. Anevertheless
  2. Botherwise
  3. Caccordingly
  4. Dinstead
解析 / Explanation
C. Why: The revision is the natural result of the request, so “accordingly” (= as a result) fits; “nevertheless” would signal a contrast.
130.The survey results show that most customers find the new online checkout process much more _____ than the old one.
  1. Aexhaustive
  2. Bintuitive
  3. Ccooperative
  4. Dtentative
解析 / Explanation
B. Why: A checkout process that customers find easy to understand and use is "intuitive." "Exhaustive" (thorough, complete), "cooperative" (willing to help) and "tentative" (not certain) do not describe how easy a process is to use.
