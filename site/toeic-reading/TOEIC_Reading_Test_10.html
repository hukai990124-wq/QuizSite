PART 5 — Incomplete Sentences（101–130）

Directions: A word or phrase is missing in each of the sentences below. Four answer choices are given below each sentence. Select the best answer to complete the sentence. Then mark the letter (A), (B), (C), or (D) on your answer sheet.
101.The factory’s new packaging line was installed ahead of schedule; _____ , production costs fell by nearly 8 percent in the first month.
  1. Anevertheless
  2. Botherwise
  3. Cinstead
  4. Dconsequently
解析 / Explanation
D. Why: The fall in costs is a result of the early installation, so “consequently” is correct; “nevertheless” would wrongly signal a contrast between two positive facts.
102._____ rising fuel prices, Vandermeer Logistics has raised its delivery charges for the first time in four years.
  1. ADue to
  2. BIn spite of
  3. CInstead of
  4. DAccording to
解析 / Explanation
A. Why: Rising fuel prices are the reason for the higher charges, so “Due to” fits; “In spite of” would illogically present the price rise as an obstacle to raising charges.
103.Several _____ customers wrote to the store manager after the weekend sale ended earlier than advertised.
  1. Adisappointing
  2. Bdisappoint
  3. Cdisappointed
  4. Ddisappointment
解析 / Explanation
C. Why: Customers who feel an emotion are described with the -ed participle, so “disappointed” is correct; “disappointing” would describe the thing that causes the feeling, such as the sale.
104.The walking tour includes a stop at the old harbor, _____ visitors can sample fresh seafood from local vendors.
  1. Awhich
  2. Bwhen
  3. Cwhere
  4. Dwhat
解析 / Explanation
C. Why: The clause after the gap is complete and describes a place (the harbor), so the relative adverb “where” is needed; “which” would require a missing subject or object in the clause.
105.The new staff scheduling software was _____ adopted by all twelve of the chain’s restaurants within a month.
  1. Asuccessfully
  2. Bsuccess
  3. Csuccessful
  4. Dsucceed
解析 / Explanation
A. Why: An adverb is needed to modify the passive verb “was adopted”, so “successfully” is correct; “successful” is an adjective and cannot modify a verb.
106.Please keep your original receipt, as it is required for any _____ or exchange of merchandise.
  1. Adiscount
  2. Bdeposit
  3. Crefund
  4. Destimate
解析 / Explanation
C. Why: A receipt is needed to return goods for a “refund or exchange”, which is the standard collocation; a “discount” or “deposit” is not something a customer obtains by returning merchandise.
107.Ms. Albescu prefers to review the quarterly sales figures _____ before presenting them to the board of directors.
  1. Aher
  2. Bshe
  3. Cherself
  4. Dhers
解析 / Explanation
C. Why: The reflexive pronoun “herself” is used for emphasis to mean that Ms. Albescu does the review personally; “her” and “hers” cannot stand alone after the object here.
108.By the time the new assembly plant opens next spring, the company _____ more than 300 additional workers.
  1. Ahas hired
  2. Bhired
  3. Chad hired
  4. Dwill have hired
解析 / Explanation
D. Why: “By the time … opens next spring” refers to an action completed before a future point, so the future perfect “will have hired” is required; “had hired” refers to the past.
109.The _____ of the new visitor center has been postponed until the city approves the revised building plans.
  1. Aconstruct
  2. Bconstruction
  3. Cconstructive
  4. Dconstructed
解析 / Explanation
B. Why: After “The” and before “of”, a noun is required, so “construction” is correct; the other choices are a verb, an adjective and a participle.
110._____ employee attending the safety seminar at the pharmaceutical plant must sign in at the front desk.
  1. AAll
  2. BEvery
  3. CMost
  4. DMany
解析 / Explanation
B. Why: The singular noun “employee” and singular verb pattern require “Every”; “All”, “Most” and “Many” must be followed by a plural noun such as “employees”.
111.The Riverside Farmers Market is open every Saturday _____ 7:00 A.M. until noon from May through October.
  1. Afrom
  2. Bsince
  3. Cat
  4. Dby
解析 / Explanation
A. Why: “From … until …” marks the start and end of a period, so “from” is correct; “since” is used with a past starting point and a perfect tense.
112.Each of the rental cars at our airport locations _____ inspected for damage before it is released to a customer.
  1. Aare
  2. Bwere
  3. Cbeing
  4. Dis
解析 / Explanation
D. Why: The subject is “Each”, which is singular, so “is” is required; “are” is the trap that agrees with the nearby plural noun “cars”.
113.Visitors to the Kestrel Pharmaceuticals research laboratory must _____ a valid photo ID at the security desk.
  1. Aexpress
  2. Bperform
  3. Cattend
  4. Dpresent
解析 / Explanation
D. Why: One “presents” identification when asked to show it, so “present” fits; “express”, “perform” and “attend” do not collocate with “a photo ID”.
114.The new staff fitness room is located directly _____ the main lobby, on the second floor of the building.
  1. Aamong
  2. Babove
  3. Cbetween
  4. Dalong
解析 / Explanation
B. Why: The fitness room is on the floor over the lobby, so “above” is correct; “between” would need two reference points and “among” needs a group.
115.The company’s new return policy aims to _____ the time required to process customer refunds.
  1. Ashorten
  2. Bshort
  3. Cshortly
  4. Dshortage
解析 / Explanation
A. Why: After “aims to”, a base-form verb that can take the object “the time” is required, so “shorten” is correct; the other choices are an adjective, an adverb and a noun.
116.Mr. Vaillancourt will lead the factory tour _____ Ms. Oyelaran is attending the trade fair in Frankfurt.
  1. Awhile
  2. Bduring
  3. Cdespite
  4. Dwhether
解析 / Explanation
A. Why: A conjunction is needed before the clause “Ms. Oyelaran is attending …”, and “while” shows the two events happen at the same time; “during” and “despite” are prepositions and cannot introduce a clause.
117.Customers have praised the new line of kitchen appliances for its _____ design and low energy use.
  1. Aattract
  2. Battraction
  3. Cattractively
  4. Dattractive
解析 / Explanation
D. Why: An adjective is needed to describe the noun “design”, so “attractive” is correct; “attractively” is an adverb and “attract” is a verb.
118.The travel agency recommends _____ hotel reservations at least two months in advance during the festival season.
  1. Amaking
  2. Bto make
  3. Cmake
  4. Dmade
解析 / Explanation
A. Why: “Recommend” is followed by a gerund when there is no personal object, so “making” is correct; “recommends to make” is a common learner error.
119._____ its popular morning news program, Channel 9 will also begin broadcasting a weekly business show in March.
  1. ABecause of
  2. BAs long as
  3. CIn addition to
  4. DExcept for
解析 / Explanation
C. Why: The word “also” shows the business show will be added to the news program, so “In addition to” is correct; “As long as” is a conjunction and cannot come before a noun phrase.
120.Tickets for the Lakeshore Food Festival are expected to sell out quickly, so early booking is _____ recommended.
  1. Adeeply
  2. Bhighly
  3. Ctightly
  4. Dheavily
解析 / Explanation
B. Why: “Highly recommended” is the fixed collocation meaning strongly advised; “deeply”, “tightly” and “heavily” do not combine with “recommended”.
121.Applicants for the parts manager position should have a _____ knowledge of automotive parts and accessories.
  1. Alengthy
  2. Bgenerous
  3. Csteady
  4. Dthorough
解析 / Explanation
D. Why: “A thorough knowledge” means a complete and detailed knowledge, which is the natural collocation; “lengthy” describes duration and “generous” describes amounts or people.
122.Orders placed through our website are _____ ready for pickup within two hours of confirmation.
  1. Aevenly
  2. Bbriefly
  3. Ctypically
  4. Dmutually
解析 / Explanation
C. Why: “Typically” (= usually) fits a general statement about how soon orders are ready. “Briefly” describes duration (ready briefly?), and “evenly” and “mutually” do not collocate with “ready for pickup.”
123.Ms. Khoury _____ as head chef at the Marigold Bistro ever since it first opened in 2019.
  1. Aworks
  2. Bwas working
  3. Chad worked
  4. Dhas worked
解析 / Explanation
D. Why: “Ever since it first opened in 2019” describes a situation continuing up to now, so the present perfect “has worked” is required. Trap: “had worked” (past perfect) needs a later past reference point, which the sentence does not have.
124.If the shipment of spare parts _____ by Friday, the repair work on the delivery trucks would begin on Monday.
  1. Aarrives
  2. Barrived
  3. Cwill arrive
  4. Dhad arrived
解析 / Explanation
B. Why: The main clause uses “would begin”, so the if-clause needs the simple past “arrived” (second conditional); “had arrived” would require “would have begun”.
125.All returned merchandise is _____ inspected before it is placed back on the store shelves.
  1. Ahighly
  2. Bthoroughly
  3. Cgreatly
  4. Dwidely
解析 / Explanation
B. Why: “Thoroughly inspected” (= checked carefully and completely) is the natural collocation. “Highly” goes with adjectives like “recommended,” “greatly” with verbs of change like “improved,” and “widely” with “used” or “known.”
126.The hotel’s rooftop restaurant will close for renovations in July; _____ , guests may enjoy breakfast in the lobby café.
  1. Ain the meantime
  2. Botherwise
  3. Cin contrast
  4. Dlikewise
解析 / Explanation
A. Why: The café is offered during the period of the closure, so “in the meantime” is correct; “otherwise” would mean “if the restaurant does not close”, which is illogical.
127.The Alderton plant produced _____ more vehicles last quarter than any other factory in the region.
  1. Avery
  2. Bconsiderably
  3. Csuch
  4. Dso
解析 / Explanation
B. Why: A comparative (“more … than”) is intensified with “considerably,” “much,” or “far.” Trap: “very more” is a common learner error — “very” cannot modify a comparative; “such” and “so” do not work before “more” here.
128.Staff members who wish to attend the training workshop should submit their registration forms _____ Wednesday, 4 March.
  1. Auntil
  2. Bby
  3. Cwithin
  4. Dsince
解析 / Explanation
B. Why: A deadline for a one-time action (“submit”) takes “by.” Trap: “until” is used for a continuing state (“The office is open until Wednesday”); “within” needs a period of time, and “since” needs a perfect tense.
129.The festival organizers decided to move the concert indoors _____ forecasts of heavy rain for the weekend.
  1. Ain light of
  2. Beven though
  3. Cprovided that
  4. Dso that
解析 / Explanation
A. Why: The gap is followed by a noun phrase (“forecasts of heavy rain”), so a preposition is needed; “in light of” (= because of) gives the reason. “Even though,” “provided that” and “so that” are conjunctions and must be followed by a clause.
130.The results of the customer satisfaction survey will be _____ to all department heads once the analysis is complete.
  1. Ainformed
  2. Bnotified
  3. Cdistributed
  4. Dacquainted
解析 / Explanation
C. Why: Results (things) are “distributed to” people. Trap: “informed” and “notified” take a person as their object (“department heads will be informed of the results”), so “the results will be informed” is wrong; “acquainted” also needs a person.
