PART 1 — Photographs（1–6）

Directions: For each question, you will listen to four short statements about a photograph. Select the statement that best describes what is happening in the picture.
Question 1
听 4 句陈述，选出最符合照片的一句（只读一遍）
1.Choose the statement that best describes the photograph.
  1. AThey're examining a scale model of a building.
  2. BThey're rolling up large blueprints.
  3. CThey're assembling office chairs.
  4. DThey're hanging framed photos on the wall.
录音原文 / 参考描述
照片内容：In a bright architecture studio, three colleagues gather around a table where a detailed scale model of a building sits; one woman points at a tiny rooftop while the others lean in to look.
A:They're examining a scale model of a building.
B:They're rolling up large blueprints.
C:They're assembling office chairs.
D:They're hanging framed photos on the wall.
解析 / Explanation
A. The colleagues are examining a scale model of a building, so (A) is correct. They are not rolling up blueprints, assembling chairs, or hanging photos.
