PART 1 — Photographs（1–6）

Directions: For each question, you will listen to four short statements about a photograph. Select the statement that best describes what is happening in the picture.
Question 1
听 4 句陈述，选出最符合照片的一句（只读一遍）
1.Choose the statement that best describes the photograph.
  1. AShe's hanging a picture on the wall.
  2. BShe's painting the wall with a roller.
  3. CShe's cleaning the window glass.
  4. DShe's moving furniture out of the room.
录音原文 / 参考描述
照片内容：A woman stands on a small stepladder in an empty room, running a paint roller up and down a wall; an open paint can rests on the floor beside her.
A:She's hanging a picture on the wall.
B:She's painting the wall with a roller.
C:She's cleaning the window glass.
D:She's moving furniture out of the room.
解析 / Explanation
B. The woman is rolling paint onto the wall, so (B) is correct. She is not hanging a picture, cleaning a window, or moving furniture.
