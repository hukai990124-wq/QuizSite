PART 1 — Photographs（1–6）

Directions: For each question, you will listen to four short statements about a photograph. Select the statement that best describes what is happening in the picture.
Question 1
听 4 句陈述，选出最符合照片的一句（只读一遍）
1.Choose the statement that best describes the photograph.
  1. AHe's sealing an envelope at a desk.
  2. BHe's placing mail into compartments.
  3. CHe's weighing a parcel on a scale.
  4. DHe's pushing a cart down a hallway.
录音原文 / 参考描述
照片内容：In a small company mailroom, a young man in a gray sweater stands facing a wall of wooden pigeonhole slots, sliding envelopes one by one into the labeled compartments while holding a stack of letters in his other hand.
A:He's sealing an envelope at a desk.
B:He's placing mail into compartments.
C:He's weighing a parcel on a scale.
D:He's pushing a cart down a hallway.
解析 / Explanation
B. The man is sliding envelopes into the labeled slots on the wall, so (B) is correct. He is standing at the slots, not sealing envelopes at a desk (A), weighing a parcel (C), or pushing a cart (D).
