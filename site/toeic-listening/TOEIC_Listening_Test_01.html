PART 1 — Photographs（1–6）

Directions: For each question, you will listen to four short statements about a photograph. Select the statement that best describes what is happening in the picture.
Question 1
听 4 句陈述，选出最符合照片的一句（只读一遍）
1.Choose the statement that best describes the photograph.
  1. AHe's putting on a safety vest.
  2. BHe's climbing a ladder to the roof.
  3. CHe's repairing a piece of equipment.
  4. DHe's holding some rolled-up documents.
录音原文 / 参考描述
照片内容：A man in a hard hat and safety vest stands on a construction site, holding a set of rolled-up blueprints under one arm while looking up at a partially built structure.
A:He's putting on a safety vest.
B:He's climbing a ladder to the roof.
C:He's repairing a piece of equipment.
D:He's holding some rolled-up documents.
解析 / Explanation
D. He is holding rolled-up blueprints, so (D) is correct. He is already wearing the vest, not putting it on, and he is neither climbing nor repairing anything.

  2. BA customer is paying at the register.
  3. CThe barista is handing a cup to a customer.
  4. DThe pastries are being placed in the case.
