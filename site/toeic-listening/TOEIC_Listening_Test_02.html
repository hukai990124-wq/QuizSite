PART 1 — Photographs（1–6）

Directions: For each question, you will listen to four short statements about a photograph. Select the statement that best describes what is happening in the picture.
Question 1
听 4 句陈述，选出最符合照片的一句（只读一遍）
1.Choose the statement that best describes the photograph.
  1. AShe's watering the plants in the window.
  2. BShe's sweeping the floor of the shop.
  3. CShe's paying for a purchase at the register.
  4. DShe's arranging a bouquet of flowers.
录音原文 / 参考描述
照片内容：Inside a flower shop, a woman wearing an apron stands at the counter arranging a bouquet of roses and greenery; metal buckets full of cut flowers line the wall behind her.
A:She's watering the plants in the window.
B:She's sweeping the floor of the shop.
C:She's paying for a purchase at the register.
D:She's arranging a bouquet of flowers.
解析 / Explanation
D. The woman is arranging a bouquet at the counter, so (D) is correct. She is not watering plants, paying, or sweeping.
